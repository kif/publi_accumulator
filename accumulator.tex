\section{Applications}
\label{sec:applications}

\subsection{Saxs}
\label{sec:saxs}
The BM29 beamline at the ESRF is an experimental end-station specialized in liquid small angle scattering of proteins or other biological macromolecules.
The development described in this document are already integrated as part of the dahu pipelines running the online analysis of data of this beamline \cite{Kieffer2022}.
\subsubsection{Meniscus detection}
Bubbles in the capilary or a meniscus (not enough solution) lead to streaks in the scattering image which can easily be spotted by a sharp increase 
in the level of spottiness (as calculated in \ref{spottiness}). 
Such frames are automatically flaggued as dubious and excluded from any further analysis.
\subsubsection{Frame averaging}
Since the sample is assumed to be a liquid, thus the scattering pattern perfectly isotropic, the Poisson error-model is providing smoother 
uncertainty-estimations.
Frames are integrated with the accumulator values saved in the HDF5 file, which allows subsequent renormalization of signal (correction for the beam-stop diode noise).
In sample-changer mode, those frames are compared for radiation damage and merged accordingly.
In HPLC-mode, frames are compared to the first eigenvector of the SVD decomposition and subsequently merged using accumulators to provide a very good
measurement of the buffer.
